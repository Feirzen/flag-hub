## You were right about the catch

The Spotify connection I have only makes playlists from a description ("songs like X"). It can't add exact songs in exact order, so it would make the scrambled version you were worried about. Not worth it for setlists.

## The clean way (about 2 minutes per show)

1. On your iPhone, get **Setify** or **Setlistify** (both free on the App Store). Both pull a show's real setlist from setlist.fm and build the Spotify playlist in order.
2. Search the artist, pick your show by date and city, tap create.
3. Name them the same way so they group together, like "Live: Owl City, 2025 Provo".

Soundiiz (website) does the same thing if you'd rather do it on the PC.
