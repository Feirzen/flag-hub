# Family filter: the running list

Updated Oct 23 with your notes and the M-rated games from your RetroBat Wishlist. This is a living list: add to it any time with a flag on RetroBat ("hide X in family mode" or "keep X showing"), and the next run updates it here.

## How the filter works
- **Family mode** is one switch in the wizard (and on the website library). When it's on, the games below just don't show up. Flip it off and everything's back.
- **Anything rated M hides on its own**, even if it never gets added here.
- Nothing gets deleted or moved. Games and saves stay exactly where they are.
