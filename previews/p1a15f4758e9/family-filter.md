I went through all 168 games in your RetroBat Wizard library. **None of them are rated M right now**, so the M rule only kicks in once you add those new games. Below is everything rated Teen, sorted by how likely it is to feel too much around younger people.

## How the filter would work
- **Family mode** is one switch in the wizard (and on the website library). When it's on, hidden games just don't show up. Flip it off and everything's back.
- **Anything rated M hides automatically.** No list needed for those.
- **Plus your own list** below, for games that aren't M but feel like they should be.
- Nothing gets deleted or moved. Your games and saves stay exactly where they are.
